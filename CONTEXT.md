# Context

Definitions of the terms this project uses. Definitions only.

- **Artistic feel**: the three things that give the site its character, and that each redesign keeps. (1) The artist's artwork: the hero illustration, the cartoon logo, and the pictures. (2) The dark stage: a near-black background that makes the colour of the artwork stand out. (3) One accent colour, the red-orange of the logo. The font, the shapes, the layout, and the spacing are not part of it.
- **Design standard**: beauty is at the intersection of function and form, and neither one is sacrificed for the other. A change that adds a step or a distraction for a person who wants to buy fails the standard.
- **Study**: a throwaway page in `docs/design-studies/` that shows a design decision. A study is the visual reference for a requirement. It is not site code.
- **Panel**: a picture or a block with a thick ink outline and a hard shadow. A picture panel has a small tilt.
- **Draw effect**: the full entrance of a panel. The outline draws itself, the picture arrives as a grey sketch and takes its colour, the shadow lands, and the panel tips into its tilt.
- **Wall**: the gallery page layout. Picture panels of different heights in columns.
- **Large view**: one gallery picture shown large over the wall, with the next and the previous picture one press away.
- **Burst**: the spiked comic shape that holds the "Get a quote" button in the large view.
- **Share card**: the picture that a messaging app shows when a person shares a link to the site. It is 1200 by 630 pixels.
- **Quote form**: the three-step form on the Services page. It replaces the four-step form.
- **Phone layout**: the layout of a page on a screen narrower than 768 pixels.
- **Wide layout**: the layout of a page on a screen 768 pixels wide or wider.
- **Band**: the red strip below the logo in the phone layout of the home page. It holds the service area.
- **Speech bubble**: a customer quote in a white balloon with a tail. "Word balloon" is the artist's term for the same thing.
- **Logo yellow**: the yellow of the cartoon logo. It is not an accent colour of the site.
